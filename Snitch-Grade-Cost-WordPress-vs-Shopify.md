## Quick verdict

| If the brand is… | Recommend | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Launching / 0–₹50L monthly revenue | **Shopify (Shopify plan → Advanced)** | Fastest to launch, reliable, low ops burden, built‑in scale |
| Wants full ownership & lower long‑term platform fees, has dev support | **WooCommerce (WordPress)** | No revenue share, own the code/data, cheaper at high volume |
| Scaling like the real Snitch (high traffic, flash drops) | **Shopify Plus** | Enterprise checkout, reliability at sale‑day spikes |

> **Fact for context:** Snitch runs on **Shopify Plus**. A Shopify build is the closest
> like‑for‑like to Snitch; WooCommerce matches the *experience* while removing per‑order
> platform fees, in exchange for more hosting/maintenance ownership.

## What "Snitch‑grade" actually means (the quality bar)

1. **Design** — bespoke, mobile‑first, editorial streetwear look; fast, app‑like feel.
2. **Motion/3D** — 3D hero scene + scroll animations + optional 360° product viewer (GSAP + Three.js/Spline).
3. **Performance** — sub‑2.5s loads, 90+ Lighthouse mobile, CDN + image optimisation.
4. **Commerce depth** — variations, inventory, offers/flash sales, COD, multiple payment modes.
5. **Logistics** — courier integration, AWB, tracking, status notifications.
6. **Conversion** — reviews, wishlist, abandoned‑cart, WhatsApp/SMS, upsell.

Both platforms can hit this bar. The difference is **cost structure**, not capability.

## Recommendation

- **Launching now, want speed and reliability:** **Shopify (Grow → Advanced)**,
  Snitch‑grade build **₹4,50,000 – ₹7,50,000** + subscription/apps. This is what a brand
  like Snitch started on.
- **Want ownership and lowest long‑run fees, with a maintenance partner:**
  **WooCommerce**, Snitch‑grade build **₹6,50,000 – ₹8,50,000** + hosting/AMC.
- **Already doing high volume / big flash drops:** **Shopify Plus**.

**Our pick for a new streetwear D2C brand:** start on **Shopify (Advanced)** for a fast,
low‑risk launch; re‑evaluate WooCommerce or Shopify Plus once monthly orders make the
platform fee material (typically past ~₹40–60L/mo revenue).
