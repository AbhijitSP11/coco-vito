## 5. Database — What We Build & Why It Costs What It Costs

Your store runs on a **MySQL/MariaDB** database. This line covers the work that keeps it
fast and safe as your catalog and orders grow:

- **Schema design** for products, variations (size/colour), customers, orders, inventory
  logs and offers — structured so reports and lookups stay fast.
- **Indexing & query optimisation** so search, filters and the catalog load quickly even
  with thousands of SKUs and concurrent shoppers.
- **Automated daily backups** with off‑site copies, and a tested restore process — so a
  mistake or crash never loses your orders.
- **Security** — a least‑privilege database user, encrypted backups, and hardening against
  SQL injection and data leaks.

*Why it matters:* a store that slows down or loses data at a sale loses money. This is the
foundation that prevents that — not an optional extra.

## 7. The Costs You Must Pay — and Cannot Cut

In full transparency, these are unavoidable. They are **not** Viora Tech fees — they are
what it costs to run *any* online store, paid directly to the providers:

1. **Hosting + domain (≈ ₹31,200/yr).** Your site must live on a server. Cutting this means
   no website.
2. **Plugin/extension licences (≈ ₹35,000/yr).** These power inventory, offers and logistics,
   and their renewals deliver **security patches**. Skipping renewals = security risk.
3. **SSL (free, but mandatory).** Required for payments and customer trust.
4. **Payment gateway fee (~2% + GST per order).** Charged only when a customer pays you.
   Every payment provider charges this; it cannot be removed.
5. **Courier/delivery charges (per shipment).** Charged per order you ship.

Everything else (design, build, 3D, features) is a **one‑time** cost. Items 4 and 5 scale
only with your sales — i.e., you pay them because you're earning.
