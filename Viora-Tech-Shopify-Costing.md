## 6. The Costs You Must Pay — and Cannot Cut (Shopify)

In full transparency, these are unavoidable on any Shopify store:

1. **Shopify subscription (≈ ₹30,164/mo on Advanced).** The platform fee. Cutting to a
   lower plan increases the per-order transaction fee, which can cost more at volume.
2. **Third-party gateway fee (~2% + GST).** Every payment provider charges this.
3. **Shopify platform fee (0.5% on Advanced).** Charged because you use a non-Shopify gateway.
   There is no way to remove this on any non-Plus plan.
4. **Courier/delivery charges.** Per order shipped.
5. **Apps (~₹1,80,000/yr for full stack).** Without apps, features like advanced search,
   abandoned-cart emails, and post-purchase flows are limited or missing.
