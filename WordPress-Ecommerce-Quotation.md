## 1. Project Understanding

You want to launch a **direct‑to‑consumer (D2C) streetwear clothing store** with a
modern, app‑like shopping experience — the polish and conversion focus of **Snitch**,
and the broad commerce feature set people associate with **Meesho**.

The platform will be built on **WordPress + WooCommerce**, the world's most widely
used commerce stack (powers a large share of online stores), chosen for:

- Full ownership of code and data (no per‑order platform lock‑in).
- A mature ecosystem for inventory, offers, payments and logistics in India.
- Lower running cost than SaaS platforms at scale, and an easy content/CMS workflow
  your team can run without a developer.

> **Scope clarification — please confirm:**
> *Snitch* is a **single‑brand** store (one seller — you). *Meesho* is a
> **multi‑vendor marketplace** (many independent sellers, commissions and payouts).
> This quotation covers a **single‑brand D2C store**. If you also need multiple
> sellers to register and sell, that is a separate module — priced in **Section 8
> (Optional Add‑ons)**.

## 12. Why WordPress/WooCommerce for this project

- **You own everything** — no revenue share or per‑order SaaS fees.
- **Runs on your team** — the CMS lets non‑technical staff manage drops, banners,
  offers and inventory.
- **Proven at D2C scale** with the right hosting/CDN; cost‑efficient vs. closed platforms.
- *Honest note:* a true **Meesho‑scale marketplace** (millions of SKUs/sellers, heavy
  concurrent traffic) eventually benefits from a headless or custom architecture. For a
  **Snitch‑style D2C brand**, WooCommerce is an excellent, cost‑effective fit — and the
  build can later be taken headless if the brand outgrows it.
