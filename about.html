About us

A café that refused
to pick a lane.

We could not decide whether we were a coffee place, a gelato counter or a kitchen. So we stopped deciding.

How it started

One machine, one freezer, one griddle.

Coco Vito opened in a narrow room off the main road with an espresso machine we could barely afford and a gelato batch freezer we bought second hand from a closing-down shop in Pune. The first menu had nine things on it.

What we noticed almost immediately was that people did not want to choose either. They wanted a flat white at four and a scoop at four-fifteen and something hot and savoury before they left. Most places make you go somewhere else for that. We decided we would not.

The menu has grown since. The room has not.

How we work

Three rules we have not broken yet.

01

Churned the same day

Gelato is made every morning before service and sold the same day. Whatever is left at close does not come back out tomorrow. It is the reason we only run a handful of flavours at a time.

02

The kitchen stays open

No three-to-seven shutdown. If you want a truffle mushroom crosswich at four in the afternoon, that is a perfectly reasonable thing to want and somebody is at the counter.

03

Order at the counter

No table service, no QR code, no app. Build your order on the menu page, show us the screen, and we will take it from there. It keeps the queue short and the prices honest.

What is next

The tasting counter.

Twice a week we plate the entire menu in miniature and run it as a flight — one or two bites per course, smallest to largest. It is partly showing off and partly the only honest way to test a dish before it goes on the board.

Thursday and Friday evenings, six till nine. Walk in only. If it is on, there will be a card on the counter.
